Professional Writing, B.A.

The B.A. in Professional Writing

Professional Writing (PW) is grounded in rigorous study of rhetoric, argument, and genre, preparing students to create clear and effective communication for diverse audiences and real-world contexts — skills that remain foundational as communication platforms and professional contexts continue to evolve. 

The major is deliberately structured as a flexible degree, allowing students to tailor their coursework to their own interests and goals. Students build professional experience and develop their portfolios through campus publications, internships, and writing-related work on and off campus.  The combination of applied experience and rigorous rhetorical training prepares graduates for diverse careers in writing and communication, including corporate communication, public and media relations, marketing, editing and publishing, journalism, and advocacy writing. The degree also prepares graduates for advanced study in fields such as law, medicine, and rhetoric and writing.

Program Learning Outcomes

  • Professional Writing Proficiency: Produce professional documents across standard genres (correspondence, reports, instructions, proposals) and platforms (print and digital media) that demonstrate mastery of writing conventions, including grammar, clarity, concision, and organization. 
  • Rhetorical Knowledge and Analysis: Apply rhetorical theory and principles to analyze communication as a complex rhetorical process; evaluate existing documents, identify strengths and weaknesses, and adapt information for different audiences, purposes, and contexts.

  • Professional and Ethical Practice: Apply professional writing ethics, particularly the responsible use of information; and demonstrate professional workplace behaviors including collaborating effectively, meeting deadlines, and addressing organizational concerns in communications

Double Counting

Students may double count up to two advisor-approved courses with other programs outside of the Department of English. Note: courses being used for the Dietrich General Education requirements do not have a double-counting limit.

Transfer Courses

Students may transfer up to two advisor-approved courses from other non-CMU programs/institutions toward the primary major in Professional Writing, with the exception of the Departmental Core Requirement course and the Professional Writing Core Requirement courses. If the two-course maximum is met, other related transfer courses will be considered for general education requirements and free electives for graduation. Please see the Dietrich College Advanced Standing and Transfer Credit Policy for more information.

Recommended Curriculum Pathway: B.A. in Professional Writing

This plan is the recommended pathway for completing the B.A. in Professional Writing in four years. While it is not required for students to follow this pathway precisely, it is highly recommended for students to do so, and we recommend students begin the major’s courses as early as possible. Students in Dietrich College may declare their primary major as early as their second semester. Students who have not declared their major in the Department of English may still take courses with us.

Students may also view the pathway for the B.A. in Professional Writing via the Stellic Degree Audit Application.
