Curriculum

The School of Drama curriculum is continuously reviewed and modified in an effort to provide the best conservatory experience for undergraduate students in the School of Drama.  The curriculum is subject to change. Not all requirements are listed, and units are often variable within each concentration based on performances, production assignments, and individual projects. A minimum of 360 units is required for graduation from Carnegie Mellon University. Taking more than 60 units in a semester requires good academic standing and advisor approval.

NON-DRAMA ELECTIVES:
Actors take a minimum of four Non-Drama Electives, 6-9 units each.
1

Students are required to take a Global Perspectives course in one elective slot over the course of the first two years. 

2

Foundations of Drama II will be taken only one semester in the sophomore year. Acting Students are still required to take a non-drama elective during the semester they are enrolled in Foundations of Drama II.

3

Students are required to take an approved Scientific and Quantitative Reasoning elective.  Students usually take 62-314 The Art of Personal Finance to fulfill this requirement.

4

54-159 is required in ONLY ONE semester of the sophomore year.

NOTE:

Students are required to take a course in Cultural Competency. This can be fulfilled by taking 54-362 AREPT or one of the approved courses from the Dietrich list of “Perspectives on Justice and Injustice”.

Directed Electives

Dramaturgy students are required to complete a total of 9-10 Directed Electives: a minor in Literature and Cultural Studies in the English department (six courses); three History courses (two 200-level and one 300-level, one course must focus on history pre-1900, and one must focus on non-Western history); one European studies course (can overlap with a history course); and one semester of foreign language at the 200-level.

DIRECTED ELECTIVES: ENGLISH
All students must complete the minor in Literature and Cultural Studies in the English Department54
DIRECTED ELECTIVES: EUROPEAN STUDIES & HISTORY Units
Dramaturgy students take at least 3 History courses (two 200-level and one 300-level). One course must focus on history pre-1900, and one must focus on African, Asian, Latin American, or Caribbean studies. 27
Dramaturgy students take one European Studies course (cannot be an English course, but can be a History course)9
DIRECTED ELECTIVES: FOREIGN LANGUAGE Units
Dramaturgy students take at least one Foreign Language course at the 200-level or above. Students may test out of this requirement if they can demonstrate 200-level proficiency.12

Non-Dramaturgy Electives

NON-DRAMATURGY ELECTIVES:
Dramaturgy students take a minimum of seven Non-Dramaturgy Electives, 6-9 units each.

Dramaturgy Curriculum Notes:

1

Dramaturgy students starting a modern language at the 100-level should begin in the fall of freshman year. Completion of one semester at the 200-level is required for the major. Students who place into the fourth semester of a foreign language are exempt from the requirement.

2

Dramaturgy students must take one (1) approved History of Art or Architecture course. One option is 48-240 History of World Architecture, I, which is offered with instructor permission in the spring semester. Another  option is 60-105 Cultural History of the Visual Arts (fall only).

3

Dramaturgy students must take one (1) semester of Evolution of Clothing, either 54-245 History of Clothing or 54-246 History of Clothing II

4

Dramaturgy students are required to take a total of 12 units of Special Topics in Drama: History, Literature and Criticism during their junior and senior year.
